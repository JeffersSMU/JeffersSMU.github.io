A two-person project built from a Vocaloid producer's bedroom demos and a fan's voice — songs written like short stories, sung by someone the world has never seen.

n-buna and suis have never shown their faces in a single official photo, video, or livestream, opting instead to perform behind screens and heavy backlighting during live concerts. This is a deliberate choice rooted in n-buna's belief that the creators' identities should never distract listeners from the music and its underlying narrative.

The Story So Far

Yorushika (ヨルシカ) is the two-person project of composer and guitarist n-buna and vocalist suis, formed in April 2017. Before the band existed, n-buna had already spent years as a Vocaloid producer on Niconico, uploading songs from his bedroom and slowly building an audience who knew his sound long before they knew his voice.

suis was one of those listeners. A longtime fan of his vocaloid work, she was invited to guest-sing at his solo shows in Tokyo — and n-buna, looking for a "more human" voice to replace the vocaloid vocals he'd been writing for, asked her to form a band with him. Yorushika debuted that June with the EP Natsukusa ga Jama wo Suru (Summer Grass Disturbs), performed live at Shinjuku BLAZE.

What's followed is less a normal discography and more a shelf of short novels: albums built as soundtracks to stories n-buna writes himself, following recurring characters through grief, memory, and the small ache of growing up. The band draws openly from literature — Masuji Ibuse, Jules Verne — and treats each release less as a single, more as a chapter.

“The name comes from a lyric about only being able to sleep once night falls — from their song 'The Clouds and the Ghost'.” — on the origin of "Yorushika"

A Few Things Worth Knowing

👤

Faces, never shown

Anonymity is treated as part of the art — no official photos, no unmasked stage presence, ever.

🕕

A Logo that tells time

Their mark shows two crescent moons facing each other, doubling as clock hands pointing from evening into night.

📖

Albums as Chapters

Releases are often written as companions to short stories or novels n-buna authors himself, following the same characters across records.

💻

Born on Niconico

n-buna's 2013 vocaloid track "Toumei Elegy" topped the platform's daily vocaloid ranking, years before Yorushika existed.

🎶

A full band, live

On tour, the duo is joined by guitarist Mitsuyasu Shimozuru, bassist Tatsuya Kitani, drummer Masack, and pianist Tetsuya Hirahata — though only n-buna and suis are official members.

☀️

A 2024 Anime Banger

Yorushika created "Sunny" (晴る) as the second opening theme for the popular Anime — Frieren: Beyond Journey's End.
